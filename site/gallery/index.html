Serve

store · runner · platform

A durable server platform written in Hirsel, combining storage, HTTP/1.1, WebSockets, live rooms, and deployment tooling.

  • Storage: copy-on-write B+tree with checksummed journal, concurrent snapshot readers, and single-writer total order by design (like SQLite WAL), plus tiered larger-than-RAM storage, S3/Tigris backing with fencing, and read followers.
  • Indexing & search: secondary, unique, and aggregate indexes, vector search, full-text BM25, spatial indexes, row TTL expiry, and incremental projections.
  • Migrations & live sync: transactional migrations with dry-run previews (hirsel dry-run), live diff subscriptions, and typed rooms.
  • Clients & CLI: hirsel sdk ts generates typed TypeScript clients with reconnect recovery; hirsel dev, hirsel deploy, and hirsel host manage the lifecycle.

One Place = one fenced single-writer authority by design — scale out across Places and persist durably to S3/Tigris. Failed deploys abort automatically before cutover; post-cutover schema rollback uses hirsel store restore.

Real-time media & WebRTC

pure Hirsel transport & SFU

Real-time audio, video, and transport packages written in pure Hirsel with no Foreign code, so hirsel test --sim and recorded replay cover them end to end.

  • Transport & security: DTLS 1.2 (dtls), SRTP/SRTCP (srtp), RTP/RTCP with RTX, pacing, and bandwidth estimation (rtp), jitter buffer (rtp_jitter), STUN/ICE-lite (stun_ice), TURN client and relay (turn), and SFrame E2EE (sframe).
  • SFU & containers: WebRTC SFU with simulcast and SVC (webrtc_sfu), bitstream parsers (media_bits), Ogg/MP4/WebM containers (media_container), and room recording (media_record).

Codecs wrap C libraries: opus, av1, h264, image_jpeg, and image_webp are thin C FFI wrappers behind explicit Foreign capability parameters, separate from the pure-Hirsel transport and SFU packages above.

hirsel.ai

std.http · std.router

This website is served in production by a native Hirsel executable built on std.http, std.router, std.dir, and std.file.

  • Walks its granted _site directory once at startup without following symlinks and serves assets directly from memory.
  • Runs with only the capabilities declared in hirsel.toml: Net=Listen, _site, Clock, Tasks, Process, and Stderr.
